Hoard Hounds LLC ("Hoard Hounds," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what
information we collect through hoardhounds.com and when you work with us, how we use it, who we
share it with, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Quote requests:** your name, email, phone number, property address, the type of clean-out, your
  description of the job, and any optional details you choose to give (such as property type,
  whether it's occupied, how full it is, stairs, driveway and parking, HOA or landlord rules, items
  to keep or donate, possible hazards, and a preferred date or deadline).
- **Photos you upload** of the property and its contents.
- **Quotes, jobs and payments:** the quotes we send you, your acceptance, job details, and records
  of payments (amount, date and method). Card details are handled by Stripe; we don't receive or
  store your full card number.
- **Messages** you send us by email, text or phone.
- **Technical information:** like most websites, our hosting and security providers may log basic
  technical information such as your IP address and browser type. To limit spam, our request form
  stores a one-way scrambled code made from your network address (not the address itself) with
  your request, used only to detect repeated submissions. **[If Turnstile is used:]** Cloudflare Turnstile
  checks that a real person is submitting the form.

## How we use it

To review your request, prepare and send quotes, schedule and carry out the clean-out, arrange the
dumpster, take payment, keep business and tax records, prevent spam and abuse, and contact you by
phone, text or email about your request and job. We don't sell or rent your personal information,
and we don't use it for unrelated marketing without your permission.

## Photos

Photos are used to understand the job, prepare your quote, plan the work and document it. They're
stored privately and are viewable only by Hoard Hounds' administrator through a secured, two-step
sign-in. We won't publish photos of your property or belongings without your written permission.

## Who we share information with

Only as needed to do the work and run the business:

- **Dumpster rental companies** we arrange for your job: the property address, placement details,
  and the delivery and pickup dates, plus a contact name and number if they need to reach someone
  on site.
- **Service providers** that host and run our systems on our behalf, under their own security and
  privacy terms: our website host (GitHub Pages); our database and file storage provider (Supabase);
  our email provider (Google Workspace); Formspree (used as a backup for website requests and to
  send us notifications); **[if used:]** Resend (confirmation emails) and Cloudflare (spam checks);
  and, **only once we offer online payments**, Stripe (payments, including pay-over-time options
  such as Klarna, which are subject to those companies' own terms and privacy policies).
- **Crew members** working on your job: the information needed to do the work (address, access
  instructions, items to keep, notes about the job). Crew members don't see payment information.
- **Professional advisors** (such as an accountant) and **authorities** when required by law.

## How long we keep it

We keep quote requests, photos and job records as long as needed to provide the service and keep
reasonable business and tax records. Quote requests that don't become jobs, and their photos,
are deleted after **12 months**. Records of completed jobs, including job photos, are kept for
**7 years**. **[Attorney to confirm these periods meet Michigan and federal record-keeping
requirements.]**
You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see below), unless we need to keep it for legal,
tax or dispute reasons.

## Security

Our dashboard requires a password and an authenticator app (two-step sign-in), and our database
limits who can see what. Photos are stored in private storage and shown only through short-lived
secure links. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your
information.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us, or ask us to stop contacting
you, by emailing hoardhounds@gmail.com or calling (734) 589-7627.

## Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
