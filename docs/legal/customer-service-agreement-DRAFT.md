## 1. The services

1.1 **Scope.** We provide the clean-out services listed in your Quote, which may include sorting,
organizing, carrying, bagging and loading items into a dumpster arranged for the job. Work not
listed in the Quote isn't included.

1.2 **What we don't do.** We don't provide deep cleaning, hazardous waste removal, mold, asbestos or
biohazard remediation, or structural demolition. If any of these are found, we may pause the work
until a qualified specialist handles them.

1.3 **Dumpster provider.** For most jobs we arrange a roll-off dumpster from a third-party dumpster
rental company ("Provider"). The Provider delivers, picks up and disposes of the dumpster and its
contents under its own rules. We don't own or operate the dumpster or the trucks.

## 2. Property access and authorization

2.1 You confirm that you own the property or have the legal authority (for example, as the owner's
agent, landlord, property manager, or personal representative of an estate) to authorize the
clean-out and the removal and disposal of the items covered by the Quote.

2.2 You'll give us safe access on the scheduled dates (keys, codes, utilities such as lights where
available, and a clear path) and tell us about anything that could affect safety, such as unstable
floors, aggressive animals, needles or sharps, or pests.

2.3 **[Decision: what happens if the crew can't get in on the scheduled date, for example a
trip fee of $___ or rescheduling at the next available date.]**

## 3. Items you want to keep

3.1 Before work begins, please **remove or clearly mark** anything you want to keep, including
valuables, cash, jewelry, firearms, medications, important papers, photographs and anything
irreplaceable.

3.2 We'll make reasonable efforts to set aside items you've identified in writing and items that
appear to be personal documents or photographs. However, **you're responsible for identifying what
should be kept.** Unless you've told us otherwise in writing, items left at the property within the
scope of the Quote may be discarded, and they can't be recovered once in the dumpster.

3.3 Items set aside for donation are left for you to arrange donation unless the Quote says otherwise.

## 4. Items that can't be accepted

4.1 The Provider restricts what can go in its dumpster. Commonly restricted items include paint,
chemicals, solvents, fuel, propane tanks, tires, batteries, electronics, and appliances that contain
refrigerant. The Provider's list controls.

4.2 We won't knowingly load restricted or hazardous items. If they're present, we'll tell you, and
removal may need a specialist or a separate arrangement at additional cost (see Section 7).

## 5. Dumpster placement, permits and surfaces

5.1 **Placement.** Before scheduling, we'll agree with you on where the dumpster will be placed. You
confirm you have permission to use that location (and the permission of any HOA, landlord or other
party whose permission is required).

5.2 **Permits.** Placing a dumpster on a street or other public area may require a permit from the
city. Before booking, we'll let you know whether we expect a permit to be needed and its cost.
**[Decision: who applies for and pays for permits — included in the Quote, passed through at cost,
or the customer's responsibility.]**

5.3 **Surfaces.** Dumpsters are heavy. Driveways, lawns, curbs and other surfaces can be marked,
cracked or otherwise damaged, especially if older, cracked, soft or wet. The Provider may use
boards or other protection, but **damage can't be ruled out.** By approving the placement you
accept this risk. **[Decision and professional review needed: the exact allocation of
responsibility for surface damage between the customer, Hoard Hounds and the Provider.]**

## 6. Price, dumpster allowance and additional charges

6.1 **Your price** is shown on the Quote. It includes the labor, the dumpster rental and disposal up
to the **dumpster allowance** stated in the Quote (for example, dumpster size, included weight and
included rental days).

6.2 **Additional charges may apply** if:
- the actual weight exceeds the included weight allowance;
- the dumpster stays longer than the included rental days;
- restricted or prohibited items are found in the dumpster;
- the Provider charges other fees (for example, a trip fee if the dumpster can't be delivered or
  picked up because the location is blocked); or
- the scope of the job changes (Section 7).

6.3 **How extra dumpster charges are documented and approved.** When the Provider charges for
something outside the allowance, we will show you the Provider's documentation (such as the weight
ticket or invoice) and the rate that applied. Rates disclosed in your Quote are the Provider's
rates as we understood them when the Quote was prepared; the Provider's actual invoice controls.
Approved Provider charges are **passed through at the Provider's actual cost, with no handling fee
or markup.** **We will not charge any additional amount without your approval**, and we never
charge a saved card automatically.

6.4 **Disclosed before work begins.** Your Quote states the included dumpster allowance and, when
known, the Provider's overweight and extra-day rates, before you approve the job.

## 7. Changes in scope

If the job turns out to be different from what the Quote was based on (for example, more items than
shown in photos, items in areas not included, or hazards that weren't disclosed), we'll stop and
explain the change and any new price before continuing. Additional work requires your approval.

## 8. Deposits and payment

8.1 **Deposit.** We may require a deposit to book the job, usually about the cost of the dumpster
rental. The deposit is **credited toward your total**; it is not an extra charge.

8.2 **Balance.** The remaining balance is due **[decision: on completion / within ___ days of
completion]**. Approved additional charges (Section 6) are billed separately with their
documentation.

8.3 **Online payments** *(only once Hoard Hounds offers them)* are processed by Stripe.
Pay-over-time options such as Klarna are offered by those companies, subject to their approval and
terms. Hoard Hounds doesn't receive or store your card number. Until online payments are offered,
we'll tell you how to pay the deposit and balance directly.

## 9. Rescheduling and cancellation

9.1 You may reschedule **[decision: up to ___ hours/days]** before the scheduled date at no charge,
subject to availability and any change the Provider charges for moving the dumpster delivery.

9.2 **[Decision: deposit refund rules.]** Suggested starting point for discussion:
- cancel before the dumpster is ordered: deposit refunded in full;
- cancel after the dumpster is ordered or delivered: deposit refunded minus the Provider's
  non-refundable charges, which we'll document.

9.3 We may reschedule for unsafe weather, unsafe conditions at the property, or Provider delays.
We'll tell you as soon as we know.

## 10. Photos and your information

Photos you send and photos we take are used to quote, plan and document the job. We won't publish
photos of your property or belongings without your written permission. See our Privacy Policy.
